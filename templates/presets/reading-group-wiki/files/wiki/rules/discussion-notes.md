# Discussion notes

Starter conventions for a reading group wiki:

- One notes page per text: summary first, then the points the group
  actually disagreed about, in the group's own words.
- A term the group had to look up goes in the shared glossary the
  same week, with the passage that needed it.
- Quotations carry their page or section, so anyone can re-find the
  passage being discussed.
