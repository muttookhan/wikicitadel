# Team evidence notes

Starter conventions for a team research wiki:

- Anyone may add a source; a claim enters the shared pages only with
  a claim-level citation a second reader has checked.
- Disagreements between readers are flagged in place and assigned an
  owner to resolve — never settled by whoever edits last.
- The weekly sync reads the newest contradictions first.
