# Capture notes

Starter conventions for a personal knowledge wiki:

- Capture is deliberate: a note is filed because it was chosen, never
  ambiently.
- Anything sensitive is classified at capture time; unclassified is a
  state of its own, never read as safe to share.
- The verbatim record and the compiled pages are different layers —
  compile from the record, never edit the record to match the page.
