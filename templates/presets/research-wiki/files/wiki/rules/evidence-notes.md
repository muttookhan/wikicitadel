# Evidence notes

Starter conventions for a research wiki:

- Every claim that matters carries a claim-level citation to the exact
  passage it rests on.
- A contradiction between sources is flagged in place, never smoothed
  over; the newer source does not win by being newer.
- Record searches that found nothing — a not-found is a finding.
