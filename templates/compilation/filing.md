# Compilation template — filing

The section grammar a compiled page for an admitted agent's
filing must follow. Checked by
`scripts/wiki_compile_check.py check PAGE --type filing`.

## Summary

What the filing is, who filed it, when it arrived, and the
raw record it is preserved at.

## What the filing says

The filing's content in its own terms — its assertions are
the filer's reports, stated as reports, never upgraded to
this wiki's own findings by the compilation.

## Pickup checks

What the pickup itself verified (and what it did not):
every check named, its outcome stated, unverified
assertions left standing as unverified.

## Claims

Checkable claims the wiki takes from the filing, each
carrying the standing the pickup checks earned it.

## Connections

Pages this filing updates, supports, or contradicts.
