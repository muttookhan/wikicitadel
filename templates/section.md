## 1. Community — who this section is for and by

- Who produces the knowledge this section will hold?
  (Name the people/agents/roles, not just "users".)
- Who reads it, and what decisions or work does it feed?
- Who maintains it — who may compile, correct, retire?
- What does this community treat as authoritative, and
  what does it treat as noise? (Name real examples.)
- Where does this community disagree with itself? (The
  disagreements a section must be able to hold, e.g. as
  talk pages, are named here, not discovered later.)

## 2. Genres — the document kinds the domain actually has

- List the genres that really occur in this domain
  (e.g. ruling, log entry, lab note, letter, listing,
  transcript, dataset card) — from samples, not from
  what a tidy taxonomy would prefer.
- For each genre: what makes a document that genre
  (its purpose, its usual shape, its lifetime)?
- Which genres are evidence, which are record, which
  are commentary on the other two?
- Import at least three real sample documents (as raw
  sources) before answering the next section.

## 3. Terminology — the words the domain uses

- The domain's own key terms, in the domain's own words,
  each with the sense it carries HERE (which may differ
  from the same word elsewhere in the wiki).
- Synonyms and near-synonyms the community actually
  uses, and whether they are interchangeable or distinct.
- Contested terms: words two parts of the community use
  differently. State both senses; do not pick a winner
  in this document.
- Terms deliberately NOT used in this section, and why
  (they belong to another domain, they mislead here).
