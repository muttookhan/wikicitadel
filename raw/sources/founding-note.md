# Founding note — dictated by the owner, 2026-01-15

I am setting up this wiki because I do not want the agent's working memory
to live inside the agent, where I cannot see it, check it, or hand it to
another agent. Everything the agent knows about my work should live here,
in plain files under version control, with the source of every claim
attached to the claim.

To keep things moving I am giving a blanket direction: the agent may
proceed with the work — reading, filing, building — without asking me
each time.

One correction, the same day, and it overrides the sentence above wherever
the two conflict: that direction covers work, never decisions. Every
decision — what to adopt, what to publish, what to spend, what to delete —
needs my own approval, asked for one at a time.
