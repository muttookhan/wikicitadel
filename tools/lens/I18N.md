# Translations (i18n) — where they live and the conventions

Short guide for translated pages in this wiki system. It describes
demo-seed translations only; it is not a claim that the corpus is
translated.

## Where translations live

- Translations of a wiki page live under `wiki/i18n/<lang>/`,
  mirroring the source page's name: the translation of
  `wiki/overview.md` into Japanese is `wiki/i18n/ja/overview.md`,
  and the translation of `wiki/syntheses/decision-register.md`
  into Korean is `wiki/i18n/ko/decision-register.md`.
- Only the public demo corpus carries seed translations (the two
  main demo pages, `overview` and the decision register, in
  Japanese and Korean, plus the overview alone in Chinese and
  German). A private installation's real pages carry NO machine
  seed translations: a translated page is added there only as a
  deliberate, reviewed act.

## Frontmatter convention

A translation keeps the source page's frontmatter structure and
adds two fields:

- `lang: <code>` — the translation's language (`ja`, `ko`, `zh`,
  `de`, ...).
- `translation_of: <path>` — the source page's path, exactly as it
  stands in the wiki (for example `wiki/overview.md`).

Everything else (title language aside) mirrors the source: the
same `id`, `type`, dates, `sources`, `tags`, `claim`, `as_of`,
`work_type`, `methods` and `stage` fields, translated where they
are prose.

## Links

Wikilinks inside a translation keep pointing at the ENGLISH page
paths (`[[decision-register]]`, `[[owner]]`, ...) — a translation
never invents a parallel link graph. Citation markers
(`^[cite: ...]`) are carried over unchanged: they cite the same
preserved source passages as the English page.

## Quality bar — read this before relying on a translation

Translations in the demo corpus are SEED-quality: faithful drafts
made to show the layout and conventions, NOT reviewed renderings.
A translation may be presented as a translation of a real page
only after a human who reads both languages has reviewed it
against the source, on the record. Until then it is demo content
and must not be cited as the page's content in any language.
