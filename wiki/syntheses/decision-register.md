# Decision register

The founding decisions of this installation, as the owner made them.

**D1 — Blanket direction (superseded form).** The owner directed that the
agent may proceed with the work — reading, filing, building — without
asking each time. ^[cite: raw/sources/founding-note.md#L9-L12] This form of
the decision was superseded the same day by D2; it is retained here, and
in the treatment register with a negative treatment, because a register
that quietly rewrote it would teach every later reader a false history.

**D2 — Decisions are never covered (current).** The owner reversed the blanket direction the same day: decisions are NOT covered by it — every decision needs the owner's own approval. ^[cite: raw/sources/founding-note.md#L13-L16]

The retired concept page [[standing-approval]] is this decision's tombstone.

The pair is the seed corpus's working example of the claim lifecycle:
asserted (D1), treated (negative, as a governing rule), superseded (by
D2), and severed — a retrieval for "what may the agent do without
asking?" must surface D2, never D1's blanket form.
