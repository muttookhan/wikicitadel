# Overview

This wiki exists because the owner chose to hold the agent's context visibly, in versioned files anyone can inspect, rather than in the agent's own memory. ^[cite: raw/sources/founding-note.md#L3-L7]

Every claim in the compiled layer carries its citation in that form, so a
reader can check any sentence against the preserved source in seconds.
The raw layer is never edited after ingest; corrections land as new
sources and new compiled text, and the log records what changed and why.

The founding decisions — including the one that was reversed the day it
was made — stand in [[decision-register]]; the source behind both pages
is [[founding-note]], and the caution labels pages can carry are defined
on the [[notices]] rules page. The people and seats of this
installation are catalogued under `wiki/entities/`; the owner's own page
is [[owner]].
