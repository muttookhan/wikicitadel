Wiki print artefact — declared set

Overview

This wiki exists because the owner chose to hold the agent's context visibly, in versioned files anyone can inspect, rather than in the agent's own memory. ^[cite: raw/sources/founding-note.md#L3-L7]

Every claim in the compiled layer carries its citation in that form, so a reader can check any sentence against the preserved source in seconds. The raw layer is never edited after ingest; corrections land as new sources and new compiled text, and the log records what changed and why.

The founding decisions — including the one that was reversed the day it was made — stand in [[wiki/syntheses/decision-register|decision-register]]; the source behind both pages is [[founding-note]], and the caution labels pages can carry are defined on the [[notices]] rules page. The people and seats of this installation are catalogued under wiki/entities/; the owner's own page is [[owner]].

Evidence TTL

Evidence expires unless reaffirmed (EU-N5; pattern: a per-collection object TTL — a declarative "evidence expires unless reaffirmed" mechanism). A claim is only as fresh as the evidence under it, and a page that knows its evidence ages must say how fast.

The convention

A page — a claim page included, since claim pages are pages — may carry two frontmatter fields:

  • evidence_as_of: YYYY-MM-DD — when the evidence the page rests on was last gathered or checked.
  • evidence_ttl_days: <positive integer> — how many days that evidence may be relied on from the as-of date. The form evidence_ttl: "90d" is accepted as the same statement.

The due date is evidence_as_of + evidence_ttl_days. On or after it, the evidence is due for refresh: a re-check against the sources is owed before the page's claims are relied on as current. Refreshing means doing the re-check and moving evidence_as_of to the date it was actually done — never moving the date without the check.

What the fields are not

  • Not an owner attestation. verified_until / verified_by: owner is the owner's positive freshness signal and stays owner-only; evidence fields are agent-writable because they record when evidence was gathered, not that anyone verified the page.
  • Not a deletion or retirement clock. A due page is not wrong, severed, or tombstoned; it is owed a re-check. Nothing is removed when a TTL lapses.
  • Not mandatory. A page with no evidence fields is outside the convention and is counted, not flagged. A page may carry evidence_as_of alone as a dated record with no horizon; that is the honest no-TTL state.

The checker

python3 scripts/wiki_evidence_ttl.py [--as-of DATE] [--json] [--all] reports, over the compiled layer (filings and talk pages excluded, as in the lint lanes):

  • due — pages past their due date, most overdue first, with the as-of, the TTL and the due date;
  • invalid — a TTL with no as-of, an unparseable as-of, or an unparseable TTL. A half-stated TTL is worse than none, because a reader may trust a horizon that does not exist;
  • current — pages inside their TTL (listed only with --all).

The checker reports only. It never edits a page, extends a date, or retires a claim; exit 1 means due or invalid findings exist, so a nightly sweep can surface them beside Lane 2 of the lint lanes.

Notices — the rules page

A Notice is a fixed-text label applied to a page whose standing needs a visible caution. The wording lives only in evaluation/notice-vocabulary.json; a page carries the block verbatim, never a paraphrase, and a notice is retired only by an owner ruling recorded in evaluation/notices.jsonl. This page quotes the vocabulary so a reader can check any block against the text of record.

source-integrity-questioned — Source integrity questioned

Mechanical: the page is a source page whose latest integrity grade in evaluation/source-grades.jsonl is 'questioned'.

This page rests on a source whose integrity this wiki grades questioned in the source-grades register (evaluation/source-grades.jsonl): a specific assertion of that source stands unverified on the source's own terms. Read the page as a faithful account of what the source says, not as established fact, until the grade is resolved.

claim-negative-treatment — Claim under negative treatment

Mechanical: evaluation/treatment-register.jsonl holds a treatment event with grade 'negative' whose subject.page is this page.

At least one claim carried on this page has been treated negative in the treatment register (evaluation/treatment-register.jsonl): the claim as stated was superseded, reversed, or corrected, and it is severed from retrieval. The page keeps the claim visible, under this notice, because the record of what was believed is part of the evidence.

personal-material — Personal material, owner-supplied

Stated rule: the page's frontmatter sources include material the owner supplied from their own records, marked personal in the source register.

This page is compiled from personal material the owner supplied from their own records. It is held to the same citation standard as every other page, and it is classified personal: it does not leave this installation, and no export carries it without the owner's ruling.

as-written-design — As-written design record — later decisions govern

Stated rule: a design or synthesis page preserved as written whose statements a later decision may have superseded.

This page is a design record preserved as written on its date. Parts of it may state positions that later decisions have superseded; where this page and the decisions of record differ, the decisions govern. The page is kept verbatim because the history of the design is evidence, not because every sentence in it is current.

attribution-incomplete — Attribution incomplete

Mechanical: a compiled page (type source/entity/concept/synthesis/query) whose frontmatter sources list is missing or empty.

This compiled page cites no sources: the record it rests on is missing or was never attached. Its claims are unattributed on this wiki's own standard and should be treated as unverified until sources are attached and the page is re-compiled.
